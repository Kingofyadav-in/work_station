National Youth Force — NGO by Amit Ku Yadav National Youth Force is a national NGO founded and led by Amit Ku Yadav, prioritizing children, women, youth development, and environmental responsibility across India. Type: National NGO (Non-Governmental Organization), registered in India. Scope: National, India-wide operations. Mission: To provide structured guidance, mentorship, and opportunity to youth, children, and women across India. To champion environmental responsibility and build the next generation of community leaders. Focus areas: - Children: Education access, safety, and development. - Women: Empowerment, rights, and opportunity. - Youth: Structured mentorship, leadership training, digital access. - Environment: Awareness, responsibility, and action. Philosophy: The challenges of the 21st century require a new generation of leaders — youth who think long-term, act with discipline, and serve their communities first. Part of Amit Ku Yadav's portfolio alongside Royal Heritage Resort and Jhon Aamit LLP. For NGO collaboration or support, contact via https://kingofyadav.in.
